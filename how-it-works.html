How it works

Short version: it reads your file, looks at each column like a careful person would, and lists cells that match a known pattern of spreadsheet damage. Everything happens inside your browser tab.

What this tool cannot do

  • It cannot recover lost digits. A cut-off ID is gone. It only tells you which cells to re-fetch from the source.
  • A plain-text CSV cannot prove damage. If “09/02/2020” was once SEPT2, the file does not say so. That is why CSV findings are often “probably”.
  • Old .xls files are not read. Save as .xlsx or CSV first.
  • The gene list is a snapshot (HGNC data through the HGNChelper package, GPL-licensed). Gene names change. Rebuild it with npm run genes.
  • The test damage is imitation. No real Excel was available while building, so the made-up damage copies documented Excel behaviour. Real files will score lower.
  • The Excel reader is tested on files from openpyxl and LibreOffice, not on every Excel version.
  • It checks 7 kinds of damage, not every kind that exists (formula errors, merged cells, hidden rows are out of scope).
  • Confidence is rule-based. “Very likely” is a judgement from rules and tests, not a measured probability.
